About Smart Solutions

A product company built for gaming decisions.

Smart Solutions is an early-stage technology startup building a scalable software platform for gaming product teams. The product brings together AI-assisted analysis, player analytics, decision support, and data workflows to help teams understand what is happening in their products and decide what to build next.

We are developing Smart Solutions as a repeatable B2B SaaS product for gaming studios and publishers, with AI embedded into the core product experience.
